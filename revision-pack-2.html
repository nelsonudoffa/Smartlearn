SmartLearn Revision Pack 2

21 original multiple-choice revision questions across seven subjects, with an answer key and explanations.

Important: This is original SmartLearn practice material, not an official or copied WAEC/NECO paper. To keep an offline copy on Android, choose “Print / Save as PDF”, then select “Save as PDF” in the print destination.

1. English Language

1. Choose the correct reported speech for: Ada said, “I am tired.”
A. Ada said that I am tired.   B. Ada said that she was tired.   C. Ada says she tired.   D. Ada said she is tiring.
2. The opposite of “scarce” is:
A. Rare   B. Limited   C. Abundant   D. Small
3. Choose the correct word: Neither the teacher nor the students ___ present.
A. was   B. were   C. is   D. be

2. Mathematics

1. Simplify 2(3x − 4) + 5.
A. 6x − 3   B. 6x − 8   C. 5x + 1   D. 6x + 9
2. Find the mean of 4, 6, 8 and 10.
A. 6   B. 7   C. 8   D. 9
3. A bag contains 3 red and 2 blue balls. What is the probability of picking a blue ball at random?
A. 2/5   B. 3/5   C. 1/2   D. 2/3

3. Physics

1. The energy possessed by a raised object due to its position is:
A. Chemical energy   B. Gravitational potential energy   C. Sound energy   D. Nuclear energy
2. If a current of 2 A flows through a 4 Ω resistor, the voltage is:
A. 2 V   B. 4 V   C. 6 V   D. 8 V
3. Which form of heat transfer can occur through a vacuum?
A. Conduction   B. Convection   C. Radiation   D. All three

4. Chemistry

1. A substance that speeds up a chemical reaction without being used up is a:
A. Solvent   B. Catalyst   C. Reactant   D. Precipitate
2. Which method is most suitable for separating an insoluble solid from a liquid?
A. Filtration   B. Fractional distillation   C. Sublimation   D. Chromatography only
3. The chemical symbol for sodium is:
A. S   B. So   C. Na   D. N

5. Agricultural Science

1. Which nutrient is especially associated with vigorous leafy growth in plants?
A. Nitrogen   B. Sodium chloride   C. Lead   D. Mercury
2. The removal of excess water from farmland is called:
A. Irrigation   B. Drainage   C. Mulching   D. Pruning
3. Which of these is a ruminant animal?
A. Chicken   B. Pig   C. Goat   D. Rabbit

6. Biology

1. The process by which green plants manufacture food using light energy is:
A. Respiration   B. Photosynthesis   C. Excretion   D. Fermentation
2. Which blood cells help defend the body against infection?
A. Red blood cells   B. White blood cells   C. Platelets only   D. Plasma proteins only
3. The functional unit of the kidney is the:
A. Neuron   B. Alveolus   C. Nephron   D. Villus

7. Economics

1. A market with a single seller is called:
A. Perfect competition   B. Monopoly   C. Oligopoly   D. Monopsony
2. Inflation is a sustained increase in the:
A. General price level   B. Value of money   C. Quantity of rainfall   D. Number of firms only
3. Which is a direct tax?
A. Value-added tax   B. Import duty   C. Personal income tax   D. Excise duty

Answer key and explanations

English: 1-B (pronouns and tense change in reported speech); 2-C (abundant means plentiful); 3-B (with “neither…nor”, the verb agrees with the nearer subject “students”).
Mathematics: 1-A (distribute 2: 6x − 8 + 5 = 6x − 3); 2-B (28 ÷ 4 = 7); 3-A (2 blue balls out of 5 total).
Physics: 1-B (height gives gravitational potential energy); 2-D (V = IR = 2 × 4 = 8 V); 3-C (radiation does not require a medium).
Chemistry: 1-B (a catalyst speeds up reaction and is regenerated); 2-A (filter paper retains the insoluble solid); 3-C (sodium’s symbol is Na).
Agricultural Science: 1-A (nitrogen supports leafy vegetative growth); 2-B (drainage removes excess water); 3-C (goats are ruminants).
Biology: 1-B (photosynthesis uses light to make organic food); 2-B (white blood cells participate in immune defence); 3-C (the nephron filters blood and forms urine).
Economics: 1-B (one seller is a monopoly); 2-A (inflation is a sustained rise in the general price level); 3-C (personal income tax is imposed directly on income).
