WAEC & NECO Revision Pack 1

Original SmartLearn practice questions for seven subjects, with a printable answer key.

1. English Language

1. Choose the sentence with correct subject–verb agreement.
A. The list of items are on the desk.
B. The list of items is on the desk.
C. The list of items were on the desk.
D. The list of items be on the desk.
2. “Reluctant” most nearly means:
A. Unwilling   B. Delighted   C. Careless   D. Certain
3. Choose the correctly punctuated sentence.
A. “Where are you going”? she asked.
B. “Where are you going?” she asked.
C. “Where are you going” she asked?
D. Where are you going,” she asked.

2. Mathematics

1. Solve: 3x + 5 = 20.
A. 3   B. 5   C. 8   D. 15
2. Find 15% of 240.
A. 24   B. 30   C. 36   D. 40
3. A rectangle has length 8 cm and width 5 cm. Find its area.
A. 13 cm²   B. 26 cm²   C. 40 cm²   D. 80 cm²

3. Physics

1. The SI unit of force is the:
A. Joule   B. Watt   C. Newton   D. Pascal
2. A car travels 120 m in 10 s. Its average speed is:
A. 10 m/s   B. 12 m/s   C. 1200 m/s   D. 0.083 m/s
3. Which instrument measures electric current?
A. Voltmeter   B. Ammeter   C. Thermometer   D. Barometer

4. Chemistry

1. The atomic number of an element equals its number of:
A. Neutrons   B. Protons   C. Shells   D. Compounds
2. A solution with pH 2 is:
A. Acidic   B. Neutral   C. Alkaline   D. Metallic
3. Which gas is released when dilute hydrochloric acid reacts with zinc?
A. Oxygen   B. Nitrogen   C. Hydrogen   D. Carbon dioxide

5. Agricultural Science

1. Which soil component usually improves drainage?
A. Clay only   B. Sand   C. Dissolved salts   D. Iron filings
2. Growing different crops on a field in a planned sequence is:
A. Crop rotation   B. Overgrazing   C. Monocropping   D. Bush burning
3. Which farm tool is commonly used for loosening soil around plants?
A. Cutlass only   B. Hoe   C. Sickle   D. Milking can

6. Biology

1. The main site of aerobic respiration in a eukaryotic cell is the:
A. Ribosome   B. Mitochondrion   C. Cell wall   D. Golgi apparatus
2. The movement of water through a selectively permeable membrane is:
A. Osmosis   B. Digestion   C. Excretion   D. Pollination
3. Which green pigment absorbs light for photosynthesis?
A. Haemoglobin   B. Chlorophyll   C. Melanin   D. Keratin

7. Economics

1. If demand increases while supply remains unchanged, equilibrium price generally:
A. Falls   B. Rises   C. Becomes zero   D. Must disappear
2. Opportunity cost is the:
A. Total revenue earned   B. Next best alternative forgone   C. Printed price   D. Tax paid only
3. Which of the following is a factor of production?
A. Land   B. Inflation   C. Demand   D. Price ceiling

Answer key and brief explanations

English: 1-B (the subject “list” is singular); 2-A (reluctant means unwilling); 3-B (the question mark belongs inside the quotation marks).
Mathematics: 1-B (3x = 15, so x = 5); 2-C (0.15 × 240 = 36); 3-C (area = length × width = 8 × 5 = 40 cm²).
Physics: 1-C (force is measured in newtons); 2-B (speed = 120 ÷ 10 = 12 m/s); 3-B (an ammeter measures current).
Chemistry: 1-B (atomic number is proton count); 2-A (pH below 7 is acidic); 3-C (zinc reacting with dilute hydrochloric acid releases hydrogen).
Agricultural Science: 1-B (sand generally improves drainage); 2-A (this is crop rotation); 3-B (a hoe is used to loosen soil and weed).
Biology: 1-B (mitochondria are the main site of aerobic respiration); 2-A (this is osmosis); 3-B (chlorophyll absorbs light energy).
Economics: 1-B (higher demand tends to raise equilibrium price); 2-B (the next best option sacrificed); 3-A (land is a factor of production).
